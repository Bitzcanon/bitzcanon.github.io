Toxic Tide

Toxic Tide is a 2D endless runner that has the player escaping a deadly wall of toxic gas. The player has to make use of their grappling hook to traverse through the obstacle-ridden laboratory, collect gears to upgrade their gear and survive for as long as they can.

This game is the result of a 3-month long school project where the goal was to create a 5-minute gameplay experience that emphasizes on aesthetics and engagement within a limited scope.

3 Designers | 2 Artists

Roles and Responsibilities

I acted as the Product Manager for this project, where I helped delegate tasks, manage deadlines and ensured that planned gameplay features were feasible within the scope and abilities of the team.

I was also the Technical and Gameplay Designer for the game, where I was responsible for the grappling hook system, camera behavior, audio implementation and data management/serialization for the leaderboard and other game data.

As part of a later trimester's audio-focused module, I also worked alongside another designer to compose and arrange background music for this game, where I was responsible for the Town (2nd level) and Main Menu BGM. We also sourced and tailor edited sound effects to better suit the gameplay.

Learning Takeaways

This project was my first foray into managing a team. I learnt how to cater to the needs and abilities of each team member while still meeting deadlines with high quality work. It was challenging initially as this was some members' first time developing a game, and everyone had other school modules to manage on the side.

On the design side, I decided to challenge myself with implementing a satisfying grappling hook mechanic and making use of cleaner coding habits to help my teammates understand what I was writing.

This was also my first time composing / arranging music and editing sound effects, making use of techniques that we learnt during the audio module. It was a very different work process to what I was used to but something I would be happy to attempt again in future projects.
